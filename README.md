# GoharTwin

### Industrial Digital Twin & Analytics Platform by Artavan Technologies

GoharTwin is an Industrial Digital Twin platform developed by **Artavan Technologies** for monitoring, modeling, analyzing, predicting, simulating, and optimizing industrial assets and processes.

It is designed to bring together operational data, engineering knowledge, analytics, and Artificial Intelligence within a unified environment for engineering and operational decision support.

> **Status:** Under active development. This public repository presents the product, research direction, and selected non-confidential materials. Production source code and industrial data are not publicly released.

---

## Overview

Industrial operations generate information across control systems, sensors, historians, laboratory systems, maintenance platforms, production reports, alarms, and engineering documents.

GoharTwin is being developed to transform these fragmented sources into intelligent digital representations of industrial equipment, processes, production lines, and operating environments.

The platform focuses on helping engineers and operators:

- Monitor industrial processes and assets
- Analyze historical and real-time behavior
- Detect abnormal operating conditions
- Predict process and equipment outcomes
- Evaluate operational scenarios
- Optimize production, quality, energy, and asset performance
- Support engineering decision-making

## Integration with GoharMind

GoharTwin and **GoharMind** are complementary products within the Artavan industrial intelligence ecosystem.

**GoharTwin** provides operational context through Digital Twin modeling, monitoring, analytics, simulation, prediction, and optimization.

**GoharMind** provides Industrial AI, engineering knowledge intelligence, contextual retrieval, analytics, and AI-assisted decision support.

Together, they are being developed to connect industrial data, engineering knowledge, AI, and Digital Twin technologies within a unified decision-support environment.
